Our Story

Good Food. Good People. Great Memories.

Born in the heart of Westlands, SpiceBite is a modern Nairobi restaurant where Kenyan flavors meet contemporary culinary craft. We believe food is more than sustenance — it is culture, connection and community on a plate.

From the warmth of our pilau spice blends to the crispness of our grills, every dish tells a story of our land, our people and our passion for flavour.
